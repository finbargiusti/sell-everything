SELL EVERYTHING is a Dublin-based collective redefining indie music with their vibrant fusion of grunge, jazz, and R&B. Formed in 2023, the group is led by frontman, guitarist, and songwriter Tom Kelly, whose creative vision drives their music, visual art, and clothing projects. Collaborating closely with lead guitarist and producer Jack Ussher, and joined by bassist Fionn Mac Lochlainn and drummer Oisín Cavanagh, the band crafts a sound that’s raw, dynamic, and undeniably fresh.
